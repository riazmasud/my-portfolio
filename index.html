Hi, I'm Riaz!

I'm a front-end engineer building customer-facing enterprise web applications. Most recently, I was at Augeo Affinity Marketing in Boca Raton, FL, working in JavaScript, HTML5, React, CSS3, SCSS, Twig, and Tailwind CSS.

A good part of my work is accessibility — auditing sites for ADA/WCAG compliance and actually fixing what's broken. I work closely with product, design, QA, and backend across the full Agile cycle, and use tools like Cursor and Claude to move faster without cutting corners.

I'm currently exploring my next front-end role — happy to talk about full-time or contract opportunities, remote or in the South Florida area.

What I Do:

Front-End Development

Building responsive, maintainable interfaces in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and SCSS — from component libraries to full page builds.

Accessibility Engineering

Auditing existing sites for ADA/WCAG issues and remediating them — keyboard navigation, contrast, screen-reader support, the real fixes, not just a checkbox.

React & Tailwind Builds

Component-driven front ends built with React and styled with Tailwind CSS, for projects that need to move fast and stay clean.
